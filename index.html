How we work

The RESOLVED Method

The same process for every client, every time — whether the goal is less pain or a faster marathon.

01

Measure

Objective testing to establish where you are now. DEXA, VO₂ Max, strength, resting metabolic rate, lactate — whichever is relevant to you.

02

Understand

Your results, interpreted against normative data, your symptoms and your goals — explained clearly, not just handed over.

03

Intervene

An individualised, progressive exercise strategy built from what was actually found — not a generic program.

04

Track

The measures that matter, repeated. Progress becomes a number you can see, not a feeling you have to trust.
